Governance

Conduit Community Edition is maintainer-led. Final decisions on roadmap,
licensing strategy for future versions, release timing, and acceptance of
contributions remain with the maintainer(s).

The purpose of this document is to make the project's contributor protections
explicit so contributors understand the deal before they spend time here.

Maintainer Model

1. The maintainer(s) control direction, review, and release management.
2. The maintainer(s) may reject contributions for product, security, quality,
licensing, or maintenance reasons.
3. The maintainer(s) may offer separate commercial licenses for the Project.

Contributor Protections

1. Contributors keep copyright in their own Contributions.
2. Accepted Contributions to a published version inherit that version's stated
license terms, including that version's scheduled Apache 2.0 Change License as
published in the repository `LICENSE`.
3. The maintainer(s) will not retroactively remove or delay the Apache 2.0
conversion for a version after that version has been publicly released under
the repository `LICENSE`.
4. The maintainer(s) will not relicense an already published version under more
restrictive terms than the terms that applied when that version was first
published.
5. Future versions may use different licensing terms, but those changes apply
prospectively only.
6. The maintainer(s) will keep the contributor agreement lightweight and will
avoid requiring assignment of contributor copyright ownership.

Commercial Boundary

This project is intended to prevent third parties, especially large commercial
operators, from profiting from the Project without permission. The source-
available model is deliberate:

1. use and inspection are allowed under the repository `LICENSE`;
2. commercial hosting, resale, and managed-service use require separate written
permission from the maintainer(s);
3. internal production use above the license's stated revenue threshold
requires a separate written commercial agreement with the maintainer(s);
4. later open-source conversion happens on the schedule stated in `LICENSE`.

Contribution Rules

1. All contributions must comply with the repository `CLAUDE.md` requirements,
including the project's security and testing standards.
2. All contributors must accept `CLA.md` before a Contribution can be merged.
3. Contributions may be edited, rebased, split, or declined by the maintainer(s)
as needed for the health of the Project.

Security

Security-sensitive issues should be reported privately to the maintainer(s)
rather than disclosed publicly before a fix is available. The maintainer(s) may
delay public discussion of a vulnerability until users have a reasonable path to
patch.

Decision Transparency

Material decisions about repository-wide licensing changes, contribution policy
changes, and governance changes should be documented in the repository before or
at the time they take effect.

No Partnership

Participation in the Project does not create a partnership, joint venture,
fiduciary duty, employment relationship, or expectation of revenue sharing.
